“An eater's manifesto: Eat food. Not too much. Mostly plants.”

Executive Summary

Pollan dismantles 'nutritionism' and offers common-sense rules for healthy eating.

Core Takeaways & Mental Models

1

Eat food, not nutrients: real meals beat edible food-like substances engineered for health claims and shelf life.

2

Follow the eater's rules: shop the perimeters, avoid products with unpronounceable ingredients or health claims, and cook when you can.

3

Adopt the French paradox habits: smaller portions, longer meals, no seconds or snacking — culture regulates appetite better than willpower.
