Executive Summary

Jennifer Ackerman surveys research on avian intelligence, from tool use and social learning to navigation and memory. She shows that bird brains, though structurally different from mammalian ones, support sophisticated cognition.

Core Takeaways & Mental Models

1

Brain architecture matters less than circuit function: birds achieve complex cognition with a differently organised brain.

2

Many bird abilities are specialised rather than general, tuned to ecological demands such as caching or migration.

3

Cognition evolves under social and environmental pressure, not as a single ladder toward human intelligence.
