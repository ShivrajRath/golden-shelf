Executive Summary

Eric Ries extends The Lean Startup into large organizations with case studies from GE's FastWorks to the US Digital Service. He introduces the growth board, metered funding, and innovation accounting for managing modern entrepreneurship at scale.

Core Takeaways & Mental Models

1

Run internal startups with small cross-functional teams and testable leap-of-faith assumptions

2

Use innovation accounting and metered funding instead of annual-plan theater

3

Install a growth board that kills or scales projects on validated-learning evidence
