Executive Summary

Carl Sagan champions scientific literacy, critical thinking, and skepticism as vital tools for modern democracy.

Core Takeaways & Mental Models

1

Use the baloney detection kit: demand independent confirmation, quantify, control for bias, and prefer the hypothesis needing the fewest assumptions.

2

Treat science as a candle in the dark — organized skepticism is democracy's immune system against pseudoscience, fraud, and superstition.

3

Teach critical thinking early and joyfully: curiosity plus rigor lets anyone test extraordinary claims instead of inheriting them.
