Executive Summary

Dee Brown chronicles the conquest of the American West between 1860 and 1890 from the perspective of Native nations, drawing on treaties, testimony, and government records. The book documents broken agreements, massacres, and the systematic destruction of Native lifeways.

Core Takeaways & Mental Models

1

Treaties were repeatedly signed and then violated as settler expansion accelerated.

2

Military policy toward Native nations shifted between extermination, removal, and forced assimilation.

3

The same events that entered national myth as heroic expansion were experienced as a sustained catastrophe.
